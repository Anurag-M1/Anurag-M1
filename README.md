## `05` Engineering Philosophy

Building good AI systems takes more than connecting an application to an LLM.

A production-grade system needs a complete engineering lifecycle:

<br/>

<table width="100%">

<tr>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `01`

**DISCOVER**

Define the problem, users, constraints, requirements, and success criteria.

</td>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `02`

**ARCHITECT**

Design the system architecture, services, APIs, data flows, and infrastructure.

</td>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `03`

**DATA**

Build reliable data pipelines, knowledge sources, schemas, embeddings, and storage.

</td>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `04`

**INTELLIGENCE**

Integrate models, prompts, agents, RAG, retrieval, tools, and orchestration.

</td>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `05`

**VALIDATE**

Apply guardrails, evaluation, confidence thresholds, testing, and human oversight.

</td>

<td width="16%" align="center" valign="top">

### `06`

**SHIP**

Deploy, observe, optimize, secure, and continuously improve the production system.

</td>

</tr>

</table>

<br/>

### Production Pipeline

```text
01 Discover
      ↓
02 Architect
      ↓
03 Data & Knowledge
      ↓
04 AI / LLM Layer
      ↓
05 RAG & Retrieval
      ↓
06 Guardrails & Validation
      ↓
07 APIs & Integrations
      ↓
08 Deployment
      ↓
09 Observability
      ↓
10 Continuous Improvement
